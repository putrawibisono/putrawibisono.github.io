# Creative Direction — Three Visual Prototypes & Final Selection

To break completely free from both `https://sourcid.com` and conventional AI-generated portfolio templates, we designed and rendered three distinct homepage architectures in headless Chrome (`1440×920`):

1. **Direction A — *The Personal Monograph & Field Dossier* (Selected)**: Asymmetric 3-zone book-margin architecture (`240px` left Table of Contents spine · `660px` central literary/technical reading column · `220px` right marginalia & archival figure column) set in *Newsreader*, *Instrument Sans*, and *IBM Plex Mono* on warm uncoated Cotton Paper (`#F6F3EC`) with Iron-Gall Ink (`#181614`) and Terracotta Vermilion (`#9E331E`) rubrication.
2. **Direction B — *The Field & Lab Notebook***: CAD title-block header and 2-pane laboratory logbook set in the *IBM Plex* superfamily with blueprint cobalt schematics.
3. **Direction C — *Contemporary Architectural / Swiss Curatorial Index***: 12-column Swiss exhibition grid with oversized *Fraunces* display typography and horizontal gallery plates.

## Why Direction A (Enriched with Direction B's Technical Figures) Was Selected

1. **Unmistakably Personal, Never a B2B SaaS Platform**: By opening with a grounded first-person essay, a literary drop-cap, a book-style Table of Contents spine, and right-hand marginalia notes, the website immediately reads as the personal publication of an individual engineer, researcher, and founder—never a commercial software company.
2. **Zero Overlap with `https://sourcid.com`**:
   - SourcID is a dark navy/gold B2B procurement platform with a top horizontal navbar, square monogram badge, hero CTA buttons (`Request a Quote`), and commodity cards.
   - Our new design defaults to warm **Uncoated Cotton Paper (`#F6F3EC`) & Iron-Gall Ink (`#181614`) with Terracotta Vermilion (`#9E331E`) rubrication**, uses a **left vertical Table of Contents spine**, and replaces all rounded cards and pill badges with typographic ledgers, sidenotes, and archival plates.
3. **Content-Specific Form**:
   - **Biography**: Long-form essay with numbered right-margin sidenotes (`Note 1`, `Note 2`, `Plate I`).
   - **Experience**: Archival Dossier & Specification Ledger with tabular years, institutions, narrative context, and documented systems.
   - **Projects**: Numbered Engineering Monographs (`No. 01` – `No. 06`) with ink-on-paper schematics and the authentic Shimadzu SolidSpec-3700 laboratory plate.
   - **Skills**: Technical Concordance Table (zero clouds of rounded pill tags).
   - **Teaching & Letters**: Academic Course Syllabus & Correspondence Desk.
